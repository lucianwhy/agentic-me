# Sample Job Description

## Senior Software Engineer - AI/ML Platform

**Company:** TechCorp Solutions
**Location:** Remote / San Francisco, CA
**Type:** Full-time

### About the Role

We're seeking a Senior Software Engineer to join our AI/ML Platform team. You'll be responsible for building and maintaining the infrastructure that powers our machine learning applications, working closely with data scientists and product teams to deliver scalable AI solutions.

### Key Responsibilities

- Design and develop scalable microservices for ML model deployment and inference
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines for ML model lifecycle management
- Collaborate with data science teams to operationalize machine learning models
- Implement monitoring and observability solutions for production ML systems
- Optimize system performance and ensure high availability of ML services

### Required Qualifications

- 5+ years of software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python and modern web frameworks (FastAPI, Django, Flask)
- Experience with containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
- Experience with database design and optimization
- Understanding of ML/AI concepts and MLOps practices

### Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with vector databases and similarity search
- Knowledge of LLM integration and prompt engineering
- Experience with stream processing and real-time systems
- Contribution to open-source projects
- Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform, CloudFormation)

### Technical Stack

- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes, AWS
- **ML/AI:** Transformers, LangChain, Vector Databases
- **Monitoring:** Prometheus, Grafana, ELK Stack

### What We Offer

- Competitive salary and equity package
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
- Flexible PTO and remote work options
- Professional development budget
- State-of-the-art equipment and tools

### Interview Process

1. Initial phone screening (30 minutes)
2. Technical coding interview (60 minutes)
3. System design interview (60 minutes)
4. Final round with team members (60 minutes)

**Apply by:** Submit your resume and a cover letter explaining your interest in AI/ML platform engineering.
